## What the product tells us

- **Members of the Kanz community.** Kanz is for people who have joined the community on Circle and chosen a plan. Most of it sits behind that sign-in.
- **People who want to read charts themselves.** The product teaches and explains. Guides explain how the numbers are calculated, and Studio teaches the patterns step by step.
- **People managing their own money.** Members track their own portfolios, import statements from IBKR or Thndr, and size positions to their own capital and risk tolerance.
- **Arabic and English readers.** Every page works in both languages, and Arabic is written by the team.
- **People following US and Arab markets.** The catalogue covers US listings alongside Saudi, Egyptian and other Arab exchanges.

## Who it is not for

- Anyone looking to be told what to buy or sell. Kanz does not do that, and our copy must never suggest it.
