Kanz is a member platform for chart-pattern analysis of financial markets, built for education. Its analysts follow the **Classical Charting Method**: they identify chart patterns on a listing's chart and publish the levels that pattern defines.

Kanz is not an adviser. Its analysis describes the chart and is not a recommendation.

## What members get

| Section | What it is for |
|---|---|
| **Dashboard** | One page for a member's portfolio and its risk, their Watchlist, the desk's market read, alerts and courses. |
| **Pulse** | The market read: the regime, what drove it and what to watch next, with the sentiment score behind it. |
| **Exhibition** | The catalogue of analysed listings: each pattern, its breakout level and its pattern target, in the markets a member's plan covers. |
| **Studio** | A charting desk saved to the member's account, plus practice: learning the classic patterns step by step in challenges. |
| **Tools** | Risk/reward and ATR calculators, sized to the member's own capital and risk tolerance. |

## How people join

Members choose a plan on the community site, then sign in to Kanz with the same email they use in the community. The community runs on Circle.

## The disclaimer

Wherever our analysis appears, this is what it is:

> This is technical analysis for educational purposes only: not a signal, not a recommendation to buy, sell or hold, and not financial advice.
