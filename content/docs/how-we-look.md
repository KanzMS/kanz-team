This is the short version for everyday use. The full reference is `DESIGN.md` in the product repository.

## Logo

The mark stacks the letters of KANZ in two rows. The K's stem is a candlestick and the A is an alpha (α).

- **White logo:** on dark backgrounds.
- **Black logo:** on light backgrounds.
- **Wordmark:** KANZ on one line, in white or black, for wide spaces.
- **App icon:** the black mark on white, for small square spaces.

All of them are in the Library, ready to copy.

## Colour

| Role | Colour | Hex |
|---|---|---|
| Brand gold, the main action | Gold | `#fcba59` |
| Text on gold, never white | Deep brown | `#291800` |
| Page background | Ink | `#0b0e13` |
| Cards and panels | Surface | `#10141a` |
| Main text | Near white | `#f0f2f5` |
| Positive readings only | Green | `#76dc7c` |
| Negative readings and errors | Red | `#ef4444` |

**Green means a positive reading, and nothing else.** Never colour a neutral fact green. Gold is for the one thing to press, active states and section icons.

## Type

| Use | Font |
|---|---|
| Titles and headlines | DM Sans, 700 to 800 |
| Body, labels and buttons | DM Sans, 400 to 600 |
| Figures, prices, tickers and times | JetBrains Mono |
| All Arabic text | Cairo |
| Icons | Material Symbols Outlined |

No small label above a title. The title stands alone.

## Shape

- **Cards:** 12px corners with a thin hairline border.
- **Buttons and chips:** fully rounded pills.
- **Inputs:** 8px corners.
- **Highlights:** a soft tinted wash with a matching border all round, never a coloured stripe down one edge.
