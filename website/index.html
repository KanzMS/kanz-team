Before you subscribe

What is the Kanz Radar, and how do I sign in?add

The Radar is Kanz's web app at radar.kanzms.com: the Exhibition, Pulse, a page for every listing, your watchlist and Kanz Studio. Sign in with the email you use in the Kanz community on Circle. What you see depends on your plan.

Who is the Student plan for?add

Beginners who want to learn: the Level 1 and Level 2 courses, Kanz Studio challenges, practice with paper trading and a space to get feedback on your charts. The Radar's market analysis starts with Pro Basic.

How does paying in Egyptian pounds work, and does it renew?add

Choose a plan and a period, then pay with InstaPay or a mobile wallet; your plan is activated within 24 hours at most. It does not renew automatically: to continue, pay for the next period before yours ends.

How do I pay in dollars?add

"Subscribe" takes you to the plan's checkout page on Circle, the platform the Kanz community runs on. You choose the period there and pay by card, and your content opens once the payment goes through. You manage your subscription from your Circle account.

What's the difference between Pro Basic and Pro Elite?add

Pro Basic covers the Arab markets (Egypt, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait, the UAE and Qatar), with daily live follow-up during Arab-market hours and one-to-one meetings with the analysts. Pro Elite adds the global markets, the global markets space, Western-market and metals reports, the Elite workshops with Ahmed Shaaban, and follow-up across global market hours.

Do I need a Circle account?add

Yes. The Kanz community, the Academy, the reports and the lounges all live on Circle, and your subscription is tied to the email you use there. If you don't have one, you can create it for free when you join the free community.

Does Kanz give investment recommendations?add

No. Kanz provides technical analysis for educational and informational purposes under the Classical Charting Method. It is not financial advice. The investment decision, and its responsibility, rests with the subscriber.
